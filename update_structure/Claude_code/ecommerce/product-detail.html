Industry-Leading Noise Canceling with Auto NC Optimizer

The Sony WH-1000XM5 headphones redefine the art of noise canceling. Using two processors and eight microphones, the WH-1000XM5 headphones automatically optimize noise canceling based on your wearing conditions and environment — so you hear only what matters.
